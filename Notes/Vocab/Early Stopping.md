# Early Stopping

**In one line:** ending training before it's "finished" because more training starts making the model worse.

Ai2 found DPO helps up to a point and then hurts, so they tuned how much data and how high a [[Learning Rate|learning rate]] to use. Sign that DPO is a strong push: too much of it overshoots.
