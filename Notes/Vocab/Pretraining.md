# Pretraining

**In one line:** the first, biggest phase of training, where a model reads trillions of words from the internet and books and learns to predict the next word.

**Plain explanation:** This is where the model learns language, facts and patterns in general. It costs by far the most compute. The result is a [[Base Model|base model]]: it knows a lot, but it doesn't follow instructions. Ask it "Write a story" and it might continue with "...about your summer vacation, due Friday," as if it were finishing a homework handout.

**OLMo 3's pretraining data** is called *Dolma 3*. We don't study it in this project (yet).
